ScreEnough Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Applies to the ScreEnough test version (TestFlight)

ScreEnough helps you use your phone more consciously by limiting the apps you choose. This policy explains what data the app uses, what leaves your phone, and what your rights are. In short: your Screen Time data stays on your phone. During the test we only receive anonymous numbers, such as minutes and opens per group of apps, so we can improve the app.

Who we are

ScreEnough is made by Floris Schmeddes and Quinten Johnson (the Netherlands). We are responsible for the data described here. Contact: scherm.bewust@gmail.com.

Screen Time data stays on your phone

ScreEnough uses Apple's Screen Time framework (Family Controls) to block the apps you pick and to measure how long you use them. Apple does not show us which apps you picked: the app only gets anonymous tokens, and these never leave your phone. Your app choices, your plan, your night and work times and anything you type in the app (such as your reasons) are stored only on your phone.

What we receive during the test

To learn whether the app works, the test version sends anonymous usage numbers to our analytics service. These numbers are linked to a random code that the app makes when you install it (you can see it at the bottom of Settings). They are not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or phone number.

Why we use it

Only to find bugs and see whether the app helps during the test. We do not sell the data, use it for advertising or share it with anyone else. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in testing and improving the app (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). You were told about this before joining the test.

Services we use

  • PostHog (analytics), hosted in the EU (Frankfurt). Receives the anonymous numbers above.
  • Expo (app updates). The app checks Expo's servers for new versions. As with any internet request, this involves your IP address and basic device information (such as the iOS version), but no usage data.
  • Apple (TestFlight, Screen Time). Apple's own privacy policy applies to TestFlight. Apple may share crash reports and feedback you send through TestFlight with us.

Notifications from the app are created on your phone itself. No notification service is involved.

How long we keep it

We keep the test data until the test is over, and at most 12 months after that. Then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us to see or delete your data. Email scherm.bewust@gmail.com with the code shown at the bottom of Settings in the app, and we will delete everything linked to it within 30 days. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your phone. You also have the right to complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Children

The test is meant for people aged 16 and older.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we update this page and the date above, and tell testers before the change reaches them.
